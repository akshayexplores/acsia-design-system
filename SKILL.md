Key things to honour for Acsia:

Five in-house **Solution Accelerators**: SABATON (Rust automotive Linux platform), TITAN (CI/CD/CT pipeline), SHARP (system health tracer/profiler), MAYAAVI (virtualized Linux/Android dev platform on AWS), XACT (telematics cloud backend for OTA & EV charging). Proof points: 11 years, 400+ engineers, 6M vehicles on Acsia code, 40 live programs, 20 OEM & 15 Tier-1 engagements. Presence: US, Germany, Japan, India.
- **Voice:** confident, clear, honest, expert; "simplify the complex"; never arrogant; no emoji; British/intl spelling (colour, optimisation). Tagline locked as "Technology that drives Tomorrow".
- **Colour:** near-monochrome blue — Midnight Blue `#1e3659` grounds, Summer Sky `#36abff` is the single accent; warm neutrals (Sea Salt, Beach Sand) soften. One accent blue per composition; no purple/rainbow gradients.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans everywhere (Plex Mono for data/code). Tight bold headlines, roomy body.
- **Feel:** clean, technical, premium, automotive-grade; generous whitespace; subtle cool-tinted shadows; restrained ease-out motion; no bounce.
- **Icons:** Lucide line set (substitute — flag it; replace with official Acsia icons when provided).
- **Logo:** the bundled lockup is a typographic placeholder — request/replace with official artwork (wordmark + 4 dots).
